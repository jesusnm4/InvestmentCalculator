## Priority Issues

1. [P1] DEFECT: undo after a reorder acts on the wrong season. Add a season, move it up, Undo: the retirement season is deleted. Switch E to Save, move E up, Undo: D is lost and E duplicated with the same id. Undo entries address seasons by index. Fix: address by period id. Command: /impeccable harden.
2. [P1] DEFECT: a planned expense that causes a run-out is not named and its dip goes silent. Example plan, Retirement Withdraw $1,500, + extra −80,000 at Saving 1 yr: "Runs out 2y 3m into Retirement", no "after Extra 1", dip warning hidden, no dip label, only lever "Starting with $166,000 more". Fix: name the expense, keep the dip line and label, offer the expense lever. Command: /impeccable clarify.
3. [P2] DEFECT: anchors are lost. A season moved to the top drops its extras' anchor (Gift stays at month 121, now "during Saving"); undoing a season delete does not restore the anchors the delete dropped. Command: /impeccable harden.
4. [P2] DEFECT: Until treats junk ("abc", "-5", "1e9") as blank = to the end of the plan, breaking the keep-last-value rule (number input reports '' for bad input; use validity.badInput). Command: /impeccable harden.
5. [P2] DEFECT: phone toasts and keyboard. The top toast covers the new season card's name after Add (375–390 wide) and the hero when you scroll to results; deleting an extra or undoing a delete focuses a text field and raises the keyboard. Command: /impeccable adapt.
