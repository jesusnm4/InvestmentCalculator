## Design Specificity Verdict

LLM assessment: authored for this product. The field-notebook world (paper, ledger-ink serif answers, numbered season spine, season-named chart bands, shape-coded extras) and the answer-first hero with a lever are specific to "will my money last through the seasons of my life". The input chrome (segmented pills, underlined links, blue full-width buttons) is generic tasteful SaaS; identity lives in the results, not the plan.

Deterministic scan: 2 findings, both `cramped-padding` on `div.segmented` (#theme-toggle, #money-mode), both false positives (a 2px track around pill buttons that pad their own text). Overlay (run in Playwright, light and dark): "No anti-patterns found." Regression suite: 54/54.

## Priority Issues

1. [P1] DEFECT: a withdrawing plan with a repeating expense keeps charging after the run-out, and the headline understates it. Repro: example plan, + Add extra -60000 at 25 yrs, Repeat. Headline "Runs out 6y 1m into Retirement, after Extra 1 / About $360 a month unpaid for 19 yrs"; Owed at the end $1,352,691; the chart steps down to −$1.35M filled in Retirement's aqua (breaks No Green Shortfall / Loss Red wash), with no markers explaining the steps; the repeat toast counts payments after the run-out. Fix: the sub-line names the debt ("…and ends $1.35M below zero, from Extra 1"); post-run-out debt in the Loss Red wash with its markers. Command: /impeccable harden.
2. [P2] DEFECT: inputs still disagree with the math in places the last round missed. Season and extra amount accept "1e9"; a cleared extra amount is $0 at once; Withdraw % 150 stays on screen while 100 is used; Repeat Every 0 shows "0 years" but repeats monthly ($1,000 yearly → 49 payments); fractional months are rounded silently (Length 0.3 mo → "no length"); a cleared Starting amount is $0 and blank. Fix: one rule for every number field — clamp or keep-last, and show what the math uses on leaving the field. Command: /impeccable harden.
3. [P2] DEFECT: an OS theme flip while typing in a number field corrupts it (WebKit: "8001"+"2" → "2"; Chromium: caret to start → "28001"). The last round's fix only works for text fields (number inputs have no selection API). Fix: on a theme flip recolour in place instead of rebuilding the cards, or restore number values by keeping the live element. Command: /impeccable harden.
4. [P2] DEFECT: two clocks for "when". Anchoring an extra at 25 yrs to Retirement (starts 20 yrs) shows "59 months" / "4y 11m into Retirement" (plan offsets count month N at its end, season offsets are 0-based). The baseline delta says "ran out after 29y 8m" (plan time) beside a headline "9y 8m into Retirement" (season time). Fix: one convention so 25 yrs from the start = 5 yrs into Retirement; say baseline run-outs in season terms. Command: /impeccable clarify.
5. [P3] DEFECT: hero label and answer drift at the edges. 100-year cap: "WILL IT LAST?" answered by "Retirement gets no time" while the third figure flips to Monthly income; seasons after Retirement: "Lasts through Retirement · $57,381 left at the end" without saying the end is 50 years later. Command: /impeccable clarify.

## Persona Red Flags

Jordan (first-timer): "Paid in, in today's dollars $63,111 / you put in $70,000" invites "where did $6,889 go?"; the anchor/offset clock on the extra card is where Jordan gets lost.
Sam (screen reader/keyboard): silent rejection of input is invisible (old result keeps being spoken while the field says "-500"); the baseline delta is a run-on parenthetical when read; each toast speaks twice (toast region then summary); a theme flip re-reads the whole result; swatch buttons are named only by colour.
Casey (phone): landscape leaves little editing room (masthead ~110px + result bar ~60px); a repeating extra card fills the screen.
Retiree checking withdrawals: P1 — a long-term-care expense reads as "$360 a month unpaid" for a $1.35M hole; "Now a fixed $360/month" says fixed for an inflation-indexed amount.
